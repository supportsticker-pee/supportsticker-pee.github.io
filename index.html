Make stickers. Share the fun.

Sticker Pee is a free Android app where you can turn photos and text into stickers, build your own sticker packs, discover packs made by other creators and add them to WhatsApp.

Android app

Create

Cut out a photo, add text or generate a sticker with AI and save it to your own pack.

Discover

Browse trending packs and categories made by the Sticker Pee community.

Share

Add any pack to WhatsApp with one tap and send it to your friends.

Your account

Sign in with email, Google or Facebook to save your creations and follow creators.

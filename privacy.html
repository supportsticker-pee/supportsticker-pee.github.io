Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 5, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information the Sticker Pee app and website ("Sticker Pee", "we", "us") collect, how we use it and the choices you have. By using Sticker Pee you agree to this policy.

1. Information we collect

Account information. When you sign up or sign in with email and password, Google or Facebook, we receive your name, email address and profile picture (where provided by the sign-in service) and a unique user ID. If you use email sign-up we also store the username you choose. We never receive or store your Google or Facebook password.

Content you create. Stickers, sticker packs, images you upload or edit, text, comments, favourites, follows and profile details (display name, username, bio, avatar) that you add to the app.

Usage information. Basic activity such as pack views, downloads, shares and similar interactions, used to show trending content and creator statistics. Our servers may also keep technical logs (for example IP address and device or app version) for security and troubleshooting.

Device permissions. With your permission, the app uses your camera and photos/storage to let you take or choose pictures for stickers. These pictures stay on your device unless you choose to upload or publish them. The app also checks whether WhatsApp is installed so it can add sticker packs to it.

AI sticker prompts. If you use the AI sticker generator, the text prompt you write is sent to a third-party image generation service (Pollinations) to create the image. Please do not include personal or sensitive information in prompts.

2. How we use information

  • To create and manage your account and sign you in.
  • To let you create, save, publish, share and discover stickers and packs.
  • To show trending packs, creator profiles, comments and favourites.
  • To keep the app secure, prevent abuse and fix problems.
  • To respond to your messages and removal requests.

We do not sell your personal information. We do not show third-party ads in the app.

3. Google and Facebook sign-in

If you choose to sign in with Google or Facebook, we only request basic profile information (name, email address, profile picture). We use it only to create and operate your Sticker Pee account. Sticker Pee's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

4. Who we share information with

  • Public content. Packs, stickers, comments and profile details that you publish may be visible to other users.
  • Service providers. We use Supabase to provide sign-in, database and file storage. Sign-in providers (Google, Facebook) process your login. The AI generator uses Pollinations as described above. These providers handle data only to provide their service.
  • WhatsApp. When you tap "Add to WhatsApp", the sticker pack is handed to the WhatsApp app on your device.
  • Legal. We may disclose information if required by law or to protect the rights and safety of our users and the app.

5. Data retention and deletion

We keep your information while your account is active. You can delete your account inside the app (Profile → Delete account), or email us at the address below and we will delete your account and the content linked to it. Some content may remain in backups for a short period before it is permanently removed.

6. Security

We use reasonable technical measures to protect your information, including encrypted connections. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

7. Children

Sticker Pee is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has given us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

8. Your choices

  • Edit or remove your profile details and your own content in the app.
  • Withdraw camera or storage permission any time in your phone settings.
  • Ask us to access, correct or delete your data by email.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date at the top shows when it last changed. Continued use of the app after an update means you accept the new policy.

10. Contact us

Email: supportstickerpee@gmail.com

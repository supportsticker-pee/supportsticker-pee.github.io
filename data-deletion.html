Delete Your Data

Last updated: November 5, 2026

You can delete your Sticker Pee account and the data linked to it at any time. This also applies if you signed in with Facebook or Google.

Option 1: Delete inside the app

  1. Open the Sticker Pee app and sign in.
  2. Go to Profile.
  3. Choose Delete account and confirm.

Option 2: Request deletion by email

Send an email to supportstickerpee@gmail.com with the subject "Delete my Sticker Pee data". Please write from the email address used for your account, or tell us the username and sign-in method (email, Google or Facebook) so we can find it.

What gets deleted

  • Your profile (name, username, bio, profile picture).
  • Sticker packs, stickers, comments, favourites and follows linked to your account.
  • Your sign-in record.

We process deletion requests within 30 days. Some data may remain in backups for a short time before it is permanently removed. Removing Sticker Pee from your Facebook or Google account settings only stops sign-in; to delete your Sticker Pee data, use one of the options above.
